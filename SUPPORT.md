# Support

- Use [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/nrl-ai/anylearning-oss/discussions)
  for setup help and general questions.
- Use [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/nrl-ai/anylearning-oss/issues) for
  reproducible bugs and accepted feature work.
- Follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) for vulnerabilities.

Community support is best-effort. Include your AnyLearning version, operating
system, relevant logs, and a minimal reproduction, while removing private data.
