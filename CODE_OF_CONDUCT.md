# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in the AnyLearning community welcoming and
harassment-free for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience,
or ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and patient.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Accept feedback gracefully and prioritize the community's well-being.
- Respect privacy and do not publish another person's private information.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, trolling, and other
conduct that makes participation unsafe are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers through the repository's
security contact. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may warn,
temporarily ban, or permanently ban participants whose behavior is harmful.
Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical, with fair consideration
for everyone involved.

This policy applies in project spaces and when someone is publicly representing
the project.
